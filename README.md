# ⚡ Lead Finder Pro Enterprise

Ek world-class, multi-source lead intelligence tool jo duniya bhar ke **Heavy Manufacturers & Factories**, **Chote Local Stores / Dukaan**, **B2B Wholesalers**, aur **Healthcare & Service Providers** ko phone, email, WhatsApp, aur verified website status ke saath extract karta hai.

---

## 🌟 New Enterprise Upgrades (Kya Naya Hai?)

### 1. 🎯 3 Dedicated Search Modes:
* 🗺️ **Standard Search:** Doctors, Gyms, Salons, Real Estate, Service contractors, Agencies.
* 🏭 **Industrial & Manufacturers:** Textile Mills, Plastic Injection Molding, Metal Fabrication, Chemical Plants, Packaging & Boxes, Auto Ancillaries, Food Processing, Machinery.
* 🏪 **Small Stores & Local Markets:** Kirana / Provision stores, Hardware & Sanitary, Mobile shops, Saree & Boutiques, Sweet shops, Local Dukaans jinhe Google profile optimize karne ya website ki sakht zaroorat hoti hai.

### 2. 📚 100+ Curated Industries & Global Hubs Explorer:
* Dashboard me **"Browse 100+ Categories & Hubs"** button par click karke aap kisi bhi category ya global city (India ke 50+ industrial hubs, USA, UK, UAE, Canada, Australia) ko 1 click me select kar sakte hain.

### 3. 💬 1-Click WhatsApp Direct Chat:
* Har valid contact number ke aage **"💬 Chat"** button generate hota hai. Click karte hi client ke saath personalized message ke saath WhatsApp chat open ho jaati hai!

### 4. 📧 Website Email Discovery:
* Agar business ki website hai, toh tool homepage aur `/contact` page ko crawl karke unka official email address (`info@`, `contact@`, etc.) automatically dhundh nikalta hai.

### 5. ⚠️ Unclaimed Google Profiles Detection:
* Google Maps par jin businesses ne apni listing claim nahi ki hai, unhe **"⚠️ Unclaimed Profile (Opportunity)"** mark karta hai. Agencies ke liye ye sabse aasan client conversion lead hoti hai.

### 6. 📊 Multi-Sheet Excel Workbook (.xlsx):
* Ek file me multiple customized sheets download hoti hain:
  1. **All Leads:** Saare discovered leads.
  2. **No Website (Hot Leads):** Sirf wo businesses jinki website nahi hai (Red highlighted cells).
  3. **Unclaimed GMB Profiles:** Unclaimed Google listings.
  4. **Verified Emails:** Cold email outreach ke liye list.

---

## 🚀 How to Run

1. **`run.bat`** file par double-click karein.
2. Browser me dashboard open ho jayega: **`http://127.0.0.1:8000/`**
3. Search mode choose karein (Standard / Industrial / Small Stores), Location aur Business daalein, aur **Find Leads** par click karein!
